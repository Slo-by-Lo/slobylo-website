A sample evening: thirty guests, one setting, the last hour of light.

  1. 17:00

    Arrival

    A welcome drink on the terrace while the light is still high. Linen, stone, the setting from its best angle.

  2. 17:30

    Movement

    A slow class outdoors with a practitioner chosen to suit the space.

  3. 18:15

    Healing

    Sound, breath or bodywork, quiet enough that the water can be heard.

  4. 19:15

    The table

    One long table and a menu written with your chef. Candles and flowers, with the overhead lights off.

Times are indicative and move with sunset at your venue.
