One evening, done right.

Thirty guests. Golden hour. Three parallel sessions — movement, healing, restoration — each in a group of ten, no phones. Then a long table under open sky.

This is the entry point. A single Slo evening shows your guests what your brand’s wellness identity feels like before you’ve committed to building it permanently.

  1. 4:00 PM

    Arrival. Welcome drink. Shoes off at the door.

  2. 5:00 PM

    Three concurrent one-hour sessions. Guests choose one.

  3. 6:15 PM

    Long-table dinner for thirty. Place cards. No agenda.

  4. 8:00 PM

    The close.

Three ways to work together.

  1. 01

    The Signature Event

    One fully produced evening at your property. Concept, practitioners, guest experience, long-table dinner. You provide the space and F&B. Slo provides everything else.

  2. 02

    The Residency

    Four flagship events per year on annual contract. Slo becomes your hotel’s recurring wellness moment — seasonal, themed, always fresh. Monthly retainer covers creative direction between events.

  3. 03

    Full Partnership

    Annual wellness calendar. Practitioner sourcing and vetting. Spa identity and treatment menu. Pre-opening strategy. Slo designs and briefs — your team executes.
